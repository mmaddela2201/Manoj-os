ABOUT MANOJ OS

Manoj OS is a personal AI operating system designed to bring intelligent agents, automation, cloud technologies, and personal data together into one evolving platform.

The goal is to build AI systems that help make better everyday decisions while also serving as a hands-on environment for learning AI engineering, software development, cloud infrastructure, and automation.

The first active module is the Fitness AI Agent, which analyzes health data and generates personalized daily coaching.

WHY IT EXISTS

WHY MANOJ OS?

Manoj OS started as a personal journey to learn how to build real AI agents by solving problems that matter to me.

The first project, the Fitness AI Agent, began as a way to understand my health data and receive useful daily coaching. As the system evolved, it became clear that the same approach could be applied to many other areas of everyday life.

Manoj OS represents that larger vision: continuously building intelligent systems that combine AI, automation, cloud infrastructure, and real-world data into useful personal tools.
