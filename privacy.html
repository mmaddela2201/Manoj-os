PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: September 1, 2026

1. Overview

Manoj OS is a personal AI platform designed to build intelligent tools that use automation, AI, cloud technologies, and user-authorized data to provide useful insights and recommendations.

The first active module is Manoj's Fitness AI Agent, which processes health-related information to generate personalized daily fitness and recovery insights.

2. Information Used by the Fitness AI Agent

The Fitness AI Agent may process health-related information that is made available through connected Google Health services and other authorized data sources.

This information may include:

  • Sleep information
  • Resting heart rate
  • Steps and activity information
  • Weight measurements
  • Calories burned
  • Nutrition information
  • Protein and macronutrient information
  • Hydration information
  • Other health and recovery-related signals

3. How the Information Is Used

Health information is used to generate personalized fitness insights, recovery analysis, nutrition guidance, hydration analysis, training readiness, and other coaching recommendations.

The current system processes information through an automated workflow that may include:

  • Retrieving authorized health data
  • Filtering and validating raw information
  • Aggregating and normalizing health metrics
  • Building structured AI context
  • Generating AI-based insights and recommendations
  • Creating and delivering personalized fitness reports

4. Google User Data

Google user data is accessed only after the user grants permission through Google's OAuth authorization process.

The Fitness AI Agent uses authorized Google user data only for the functionality described in this Privacy Policy.

5. Data Storage

The current version of the Fitness AI Agent primarily processes health information through automated workflows to generate personalized reports.

As Manoj OS evolves, additional storage capabilities may be introduced for features such as historical trends, weekly and monthly analysis, dashboards, and long-term coaching intelligence.

Any future storage functionality will be documented in this Privacy Policy before it is used in production.

6. Data Sharing

Personal health information is not sold.

Data may be processed by technology services required to operate the Fitness AI Agent, including automation, AI processing, email delivery, and infrastructure services.

These services are used only to provide the functionality of Manoj OS and its AI agents.

7. AI Processing

Health information may be included in structured context provided to AI services for the purpose of generating personalized fitness insights and recommendations.

AI-generated recommendations are intended for informational and personal fitness guidance purposes and should not be treated as medical diagnosis or professional medical advice.

8. User Control

Users control whether Manoj OS receives access to their Google data through Google's OAuth authorization process.

Users may revoke application access through their Google account permissions at any time.

9. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect connected account credentials and health-related information used by the system.

Manoj OS will continue to improve security practices as the platform evolves toward production and cloud-hosted infrastructure.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Manoj OS introduces new AI agents, data sources, storage capabilities, infrastructure, or other functionality.

Updates will be reflected on this page together with a revised "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions regarding Manoj OS or Manoj's Fitness AI Agent, please use the contact information provided on the Manoj OS website.
